3. Flow consistency (Little's Law)
==============================================================================
  Sheltered households on PIT night (2025): 2358
  Implied entries = 2358/251 = 9.39 households/day = 3,429/year
  Implied households served in a year = stock + entries = 5,787
  Same calculation at the implied 2024 level (/1.134): 5,103
  Plan: 5,447 households assisted in FY2024 -> sits between the two estimates; the plan's flow numbers hang together.

  Unsheltered: 472 adults over Apr 2023-Apr 2025 (24 months) vs 319 unsheltered adults on one night (Jan 2025).
  For both to be complete counts of the same group, only ~153 people entered in two years,
  implying an average unsheltered spell of ~1,522 days (~4.2 years).
  More likely the 472 counts only adults recorded in HMIS (e.g., outreach-enrolled); the plan should define it.

  Scenario (illustrative, holds inflow constant at the 2025 rate):
    cut average stay 251 -> 225 days: sheltered households on a given night 2358 -> 2,114 (-10.4%)
    cut average stay 251 -> 200 days: sheltered households on a given night 2358 -> 1,879 (-20.3%)
    cut average stay 251 -> 180 days: sheltered households on a given night 2358 -> 1,691 (-28.3%)
